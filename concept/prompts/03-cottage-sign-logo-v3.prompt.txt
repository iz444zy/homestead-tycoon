Use case: logo-brand
Create a distinctive professional game logo for a cozy low-poly Roblox homesteading game named HOMESTEAD TYCOON with subtitle LUNA LANDING. Fresh redesign: give the moon-and-stars town identity a memorable silhouette while keeping the farming/home identity and excellent legibility. Exact text only: "HOMESTEAD TYCOON" and "LUNA LANDING". No other words.

Composition: compact badge/wordmark hybrid, a broad elegant warm-cream crescent moon cradles a tiny simple terracotta cottage roof and one sprouting leaf at the upper left of the lettering, visually integrated rather than a separate giant illustration. Three restrained four-point stars in different sizes balance the crescent across the upper right. A deep faded midnight-blue arch/backplate gives the whole emblem a distinct night-sky silhouette. Keep stars sparse, crisp and graphic, no space scenery.
Main title HOMESTEAD on first line and TYCOON on second, large bold friendly custom cream lettering with a strong deep forest-green outline and restrained warm edge shadow. Letter shapes substantial and slightly handmade with excellent spacing, no moon or star replacing any letter.
Subtitle LUNA LANDING is prominent and easily readable in a wide midnight-blue lower ribbon/plaque with cream lettering and a thin muted gold border. Give subtitle more visual importance than a tiny tagline. Simple timber/terracotta accents connect to a homestead sign without turning it into a generic wood plank sign. One very small pumpkin or leaf accent is acceptable, avoid clutter.

Clean flat graphic layers, only shallow dimensional depth, plain colors with a few broad highlights, crisp contours, minimal bevels. Palette warm cream, faded midnight blue, deep evergreen, muted golden moon/stars, warm terracotta. Charming, confident and iconic, readable as a small game thumbnail. Natural town branding, not a wizard or astrology game. No detailed cottage scene, no intricate wood grain, no carved ornament, no dense leaves, no piles of produce, no shiny metallic gradients, no extruded 3D lettering, no glow cloud or shadow outside the emblem.
Isolated logo on a genuinely transparent background. No mockup, no solid background, no text beyond exact title and subtitle. Landscape 3:2 canvas, entire logo within generous transparent margins.
