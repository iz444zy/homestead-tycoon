Use case: stylized-concept
Revised plant-care visual effects concept sheet for Homestead Tycoon: Luna Landing. Six separated pepper plant studies in 2 rows of 3 on cream background. Same healthy upright adult plant, foliage geometry, camera and scale throughout. Plain low-poly matte soil discs, no rocks, clods, compost rings or granular noise. Reference supplies model style only.
Top left: dry light brown soil, small green peppers, no effects.
Top middle: watering feedback animation captured mid-motion: five large readable light-blue teardrop-shaped water droplets falling in a short downward arc beside and onto the plant base, two small splash shapes at soil contact, locally darkened wet soil. Small green peppers. This is a brief application animation, not permanent rain.
Top right: fertilized light brown soil with a sparse scattering of tiny FLAT MATTE OCHRE-GOLD flecks embedded in the soil around plant, small green peppers. Flecks are visible nutrient granules, not gemstones, rocks, coins or star sparkles; no glow, no floating particles.
Bottom left: combined watered and fertilized, same brief blue droplet animation above dark soil with matte gold flecks, small green peppers.
Bottom middle: dry light brown soil, harvest-ready full-size red and amber peppers, no care effects.
Bottom right: harvest-ready full-size red and amber peppers, dark wet soil with flat gold flecks, brief blue droplets at the side.
Keep plant green and healthy in all states. Droplets are large enough to read at gameplay distance and do not hide fruit. Gold flecks only affect soil; never gold leaves or gold fruit. No implied crop-quality badge. Distinguish fruit maturity from soil care. Clean spacious cells, no labels, text, UI, watering cans or decorative scenery. Landscape 3:2.
Layered low-poly 3D achievable in Roblox Studio, chunky overlapping parts, broad flat-shaded faces, plain matte colors, economical geometry, dusty healthy greens and bright produce. Simple daylight and modest shadows, everything in focus. No photorealistic water, bloom, magic, wood grain or cinematic lighting. This is a still-image proposal for a short watering animation plus persistent soil markings, not an animated asset.
