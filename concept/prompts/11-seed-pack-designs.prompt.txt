Seed packet asset design sheet for Homestead Tycoon: Luna Landing. Use the approved market truck reference ONLY for its paper packet material, simple produce pictograms, matte low-poly style and earthy palette. Do not show truck or scenery.
Exactly EIGHT packets arranged in TWO rows of FOUR on warm neutral cream background, generous separation. Columns consistently pepper, pumpkin, grape, apple. Top row four STANDARD packs, bottom row four less-common LARGE packs. Clear nearly front-facing three-quarter view with minimal perspective so packet faces and quantities are legible. Landscape 3:2.
Standard packs: compact sealed kraft-paper envelope with broad folded top and one very large flat crop pictogram. Prominent dark evergreen quantity numeral "4" in a plain cream circular seal at bottom right.
Large packs: visibly about 25 percent taller and wider, thicker rectangular paper envelope with broad squared bottom and one simple midnight-blue horizontal band. SAME crop pictogram language. Prominent numeral "9" inside a plain cream SQUARE seal at bottom right. No metallic foil or quality tier colors. Pack rarity does not indicate crop quality.
All eight pictograms recognizable at gameplay or minimap scale: red hanging pepper with green stem, squat orange pumpkin with thick stem, simple purple grape bunch with one leaf, red apple with one leaf. Match each column between sizes. Crop shape must identify type without relying only on color. Simple readable outlines and broad flat color regions. No text except exact quantity numerals, top row "4" on every pack and bottom row "9" on every pack. No price, rarity word, brand, arrows or floating UI. No seed contents visible, no randomized question marks, no glow or sparkles.
Only modest paper thickness, simple folds, flat matte kraft surface, no paper grain or wrinkles, no ribbons, no cloth sacks, no gift boxes, no glossy collectible-card borders. Packaging is a visual proposal; apple/grape planting-material naming is not finalized. Layered low-poly 3D achievable in Roblox Studio, matching the approved reference: chunky overlapping simple parts, broad flat-shaded faces, economical bevels, plain matte colors, almost no texture. Faded warm timber, dark soil, healthy dusty greens, brighter recognizable produce. Simple daylight, modest shadows, clear focus. No wood grain, tiny hardware, dense individual leaves, glossy plastic, glass refraction, bloom, cinematic lighting or depth-of-field blur.
